## What is HiTalker  

[HiTalker](https://hitalker.com/) is an **AI-powered end-to-end evaluation platform** for public speaking and presentation rehearsal. It enables users to get **instant feedback**, refine their delivery, and inspire audiences with confidence.  

![](https://hitalkerbucket.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/welcome/hitalker-promotion.mp4)

**Core Features**  
- **Speech Evaluation:** AI avatar generates detailed feedback on clarity, fluency, pronunciation, and logic. Widely used in Toastmasters clubs, public speaking training, and English learning scenarios.  
- **Presentation Evaluation:** Analyze PowerPoint rehearsals with **AI-extracted key messages, performance metrics, and actionable suggestions** to improve impact and efficiency.  
- **Provide Online Community for Feedback:** Users can provide their comments and feedback to each others' speech videos. It encorages speakers to interact with others and actively create speech videos in the community group. 

**Target Users**  
Learners, professionals, and speakers who want to **improve presentation skills, public speaking confidence, and spoken English**.
