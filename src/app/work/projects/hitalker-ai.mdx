## What is Hitalker 

[Hitalker](https://hitalker.com/) is a an end-to-end evaluation platform for public speech and presentation rehearsal, get real-time feedback, refine your delivery, and inspire any audience. 

**Core functionalities**

- Speech Evaluation: Provide an AI avatar with comprehensive report to improve clarity, accuracy, and fluency of human's speech. It has been widely used in Toastmasters clubs, public speaking training, and English training scenarios.
- PPT Evaluation: Increase presentation rehearsal efficiency with AI-generated key message, performance analysis metrics, fine-tuned content.

**Target Users**

For people who want to improve their public speaking and presentation skills, as well as spoken English.

## My values and contributions

Hitalker was incubated from Microsoft Global Hachathon. It leverages the AI technologies such as language models (OpenAI GPT 4o, Deepseek), Azure Speech Service, Microsoft AI Avatars.

This project has been recognized as one of the Top 6 valuable project at Microsoft Hackathon 2023 and 2024. I helped the founding team to define the roadmap, design the product prototype, and create marketing videos.
