## What is KubeSphere  

[KubeSphere](https://kubesphere.io/) is an **open-source container platform** that provides a plug-and-play architecture for managing Kubernetes clusters and DevOps workflows. It empowers developers and enterprises with **multi-cloud deployment, observability, and app lifecycle management**.
