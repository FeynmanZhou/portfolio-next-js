## Overview  

Over the past years, I have actively contributed to the global developer ecosystem as a **community organizer, public speaker, and event moderator**. From local meetups to international conferences, I’ve had the privilege of helping developers connect, learn, and share ideas in the fast-growing cloud-native and AI communities.  

---

## As an Event Organizer  

### Microsoft Toastmasters Club  

As the **President of Microsoft Toastmasters Club (Beijing)**, I grew club membership from **10 to 37 members in just three months**. I organized regular meetings, mentored new speakers, and built a supportive culture that enabled members to sharpen their public speaking and leadership skills.

### Kubernetes Community Days (KCD) China 2021 & 2022  

![KCD Shanghai](/images/projects/project-01/kcd-shanghai.png)  
![KCD Beijing](/images/projects/project-01/kcd-beijing.jpg)  

I served as an **organizer for Kubernetes Community Days (KCD) China** in both **Beijing (2021)** and **Shanghai (2022)**. These events brought together **over 1,000 developers, architects, and end-users** to share best practices and innovations in the Kubernetes ecosystem. My responsibilities included:  
- Coordinating with CNCF and local sponsors.  
- Designing the event program and speaker lineup.  
- Promoting the event through social media and community channels.

### Open Source Cloud Native Developer Day 2022  

![OSS Dev Day](/images/projects/project-01/oss-devday.jpg)  

I co-organized the **Open Source Cloud Native Developer Day 2022**, a one-day event focused on open-source adoption in enterprises. I curated sessions with community leaders, moderated discussions, and facilitated networking opportunities between developers and industry partners.

## As a Public Speaker and Moderator  

### Microsoft AI Dev Day  

At **Microsoft AI Dev Day**, I delivered a session on **AI for public speaking evaluation** (HiTalker), showcasing how GPT and Azure Speech Service can empower communication skills. My talk demonstrated a **real-world EdTech use case** for AI, drawing strong interest from both developers and educators.  

---

### Gopher China 2023  

![Gopher China 2023](/images/projects/project-01/gopherchina-2023.jpg)  

At **Gopher China 2023**, the largest Go community event in China, I presented on **ORAS and OCI Artifact Distribution**. The talk introduced developers to secure supply chain workflows and practical CLI demos, helping Go developers understand how to manage non-image artifacts with registries.  

---

### KubeCon + CloudNativeCon 2024  

At **KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2024**, I presented  the **Data on Kubernetes (DoK) Day** track. I facilitated sessions on **stateful workloads, cloud-native databases, and AI/ML use cases on Kubernetes**, ensuring smooth transitions and engaging Q&A between speakers and the audience.
