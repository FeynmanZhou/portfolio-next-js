Last November, I moved from Beijing to Seattle to start a new adventure — I had looked forward to for a long time. However, the reality was more challenging than I had expected. 

To be honest, it was so hard to get myself plugged into a new team, a new environment, and a new culture. I felt lonely and disconnected at the beginning because of language barriers and cultural differences. I was always hesitated to reach out to others because I am not confident at all. I was struggling with improving my communication skills and learning new culture. 

My friend Vivian May, who had lived in four countries and joined five different Toastmasters clubs. Her experience inspired me - I used to be a member of Microsoft Toastmasters club in Beijing. Maybe Toastmasters could be a starting point for me in a new country. 
Toastmasters is a global community where people share the same vision.

Last month, I watched a Toastmasters speech contest in Seattle. It was my first time watching an international speech contest, where most of the contestants and attendees were either native English speakers or long-time U.S. residents. That speech contest was held in a church, western style, surrounding with flowers and trees.

![microsoft-tmc](/images/blog/tmc-contest.jpg)

You might be wondering: what's the difference between an English speech contest in China and one in the U.S.? 
Well, I could immediately feel the passion, energy, and joy from those native speakers. I learned a lot and summarized three takeaways that are worth sharing here:

## Be a lifelong learner.

One thing that impressed me the most was that many of the contestants were older—some around the age of my parents or even grandparents. I was particularly inspired by an elderly couple, both with over 30 years of Toastmasters experience, who were still competing for the championship. That reminded me: life is an infinite game. So, to all the young friends—pace yourself, enjoy the long game, and do not burn out too early.

## Tell an engaging story.

People connect with stories—not just fancy words or fluent language. If you want to deliver a compelling speech, you need to learn how to tell an engaging story.  The contestant who won the 2nd place shared a humorous and heartfelt story about her personal growth in her childhood, and the audience loved it.

## Emotions drive connection.

Humans are emotional animals, and emotional shifts are the secret of delivering an engaging story. The contestant who won 1st place delivered her speech just like a Hollywood actor—showcasing diverse emotions, expressive facial gestures, and dynamic vocal variety. She had the audience hooked all the time.

![group-photo-with-a-couple](/images/blog/group-photo-with-a-couple.png)

After watching the Toastmasters speech contest, I joined a Toastmasters club in Seattle. It makes me feel more comfortable because of the familiar atmosphere, everyone seems to be supportive and encouraging.

![microsoft-tmc](/images/blog/microsoft-tmc.jpg)

Thanks to Toastmasters, it makes me feel at home. I could learn from the people who share the same vision around the world.
